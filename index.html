About

Reporting fellow covering politics and policy, with a focus on ai policy and governance

Ayah covers politics and policy at The Washington Sun.

She previously worked at KQED in San Francisco, where she reported and voiced daily radio stories for the station’s newscasts and covered courts, labor and surveillance technology for the digital desk. Her reporting has aired nationally on NPR. She graduated from the University of California, Berkeley, with a degree in media studies.
