# Instructions for Claude

This repository is a journalist's personal website. GitHub Pages publishes it from the main branch.

- Commit every change straight to main and push it, so the site updates. Don't open pull requests or leave work on other branches.
- Use my words exactly as I give them: my About text, headlines, outlet names and dates. Don't rewrite, shorten, summarize or add to them. If something is missing, leave a clearly marked spot and ask me.
- Never add facts about me that I didn't give you.
- Keep the site simple: one index.html with its styles inside it, and photos in an images folder. No build tools or frameworks.
- When you finish, tell me in plain words what changed, and that the live site can take a minute or two to update.

## Site style

Keep every future change in this style. It's a quiet, minimal portfolio look, inspired by maelie.co and samfcheng.com.

- **Overall:** white background, near-black text, lots of white space, no colours, no shadows, no photos in the layout unless I ask. Thin light-grey lines (#e4e4e4) separate sections.
- **Fonts:** two Google Fonts only. Instrument Serif (regular weight only) for my big name and for clip headlines. Instrument Sans for everything else, at small sizes (12–16px) with slightly tight letter spacing.
- **Top strip:** three small columns: my name on the left, my beat in the middle, the contact line on the right (right-aligned). On phones they stack in one column.
- **Big name:** "Ayah Ali-Ahmad" in Instrument Serif, very large, spanning almost the full width of the page on one line, centred, under the top strip. It must never cause sideways scrolling.
- **Sections:** each section is a row with a thin line above it. The section name sits on the left in a small rounded "pill" tag (thin grey border, grey 12px text). The content sits on the right, about 560px wide at most. On phones the label sits above the content.
- **Clips:** a list. Each clip is a headline in Instrument Serif (about 30px, 24px on phones) linking to the story, with the outlet in a small pill tag and the date in light grey 12px text beside it, and a thin line between clips. The pattern is in a comment in the Clips section of index.html.
- **Headshot:** my headshot (images/headshot.jpg) sits in the About row, in the left column under the About label, about 240px wide (200px on phones), with slightly rounded corners and no border or shadow. On phones it sits above the About text.
- **Placeholders:** empty spots are a pill tag with a dashed border and light grey text, such as "[Clips coming soon]".
- **Links:** same colour as the text, with a light grey underline that darkens on hover.
